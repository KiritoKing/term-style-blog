Pixel art is all about constraints. By limiting your resolution and color palette, you force yourself to focus on form and readability.

1. **Start Small**: Don't try to draw a 128x128 character right away. Start with 16x16 or 32x32. It forces you to abstract details.
2. **Limit Colors**: Use a pre-made palette like Endesga 32 or Pico-8. Too many colors make pixel art look muddy.
3. **Avoid Jaggies**: Smooth out your curves. A line should step consistently (e.g., 2 pixels, 2 pixels, 2 pixels, not 2, 1, 3, 2).

Practice every day, and you'll see improvement quickly!
