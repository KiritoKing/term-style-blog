# Pixel Art Tips for Game Developers

Pixel art is making a comeback in the indie game scene. Here are some tips to create stunning pixel art.

## Color Palette

Choose a limited color palette to maintain consistency:

- 4-8 colors for characters
- 8-16 colors for environments
- Use dithering sparingly

## Animation Principles

Keep animations smooth with these tips:

1. Use keyframes
2. Add anticipation
3. Follow through and overlap

## Tools

Popular pixel art tools include:
- Aseprite
- Photoshop
- GIMP
- LibreSprite

## Practice

The best way to improve is to draw every day, even if just for 15 minutes.

Good luck with your pixel art journey!
